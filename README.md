# Where does Victoria fall behind other states, and for whom?

## The question
A Victorian Government talent attraction and retention team needs to know where Victoria falls behind other states for different groups of people, based on factors such as cost, jobs, safety, health and PR pathways, so they can decide which groups and problems to focus on first.

## Client
Victorian Government talent attraction and retention team (in the style of Study Melbourne / Live in Melbourne).
Framed as an analysis brief for a state talent-attraction team. Not affiliated with any organisation.
Built for a Victorian team; any state's team can select their state.

## Personas
- International student (student visa)
- Temporary visa worker seeking PR (graduate or employer-sponsored visa)
- Young adult resident, no children (citizen/PR)
- Family with children (citizen/PR)

Personas are based on life stage and visa status, not cultural identity.
