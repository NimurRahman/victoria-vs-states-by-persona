# Module 1: Cost of Living

## Question
Where is Victoria less affordable than other states, and for which groups?

## Measures (planned)
- Rent burden: % of income spent on rent (room / 1–2 bed / 3-bed, depending on persona)
- Price growth vs wage growth since 2019
- Hours of casual work needed each week to pay rent + basics
